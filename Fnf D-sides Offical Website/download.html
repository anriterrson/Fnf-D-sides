iOS

iOS 13 and later · iPhone & iPad
D_SIDES_REDUX_IOS_1_1_BUGFIX_terson.ipa 430MB

This .ipa is unsigned. Standard iOS installs require a valid signature, so you'll need a sideloading tool before it'll go on your device.

  1. Get a sideloading toolAltStore, Sideloadly or a similar tool, installed on a computer.
  2. Sign the IPAusing your Apple ID through that tool — this replaces the missing signature.
  3. Install & trustconnect your device, install, then trust the developer profile in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.

Android

Android 8.0 and later
D_SIDES_REDUX_ANDROID_1_1_BUGFIX_terson.apk 475MB

This .apk needs to be signed before Android will install it — unsigned APKs are rejected outright, not just flagged.

  1. Sign the APKusing a tool like apksigner or Uber APK Signer with a debug or personal key.
  2. Allow unknown sourcesenable installs from this source in Settings → Security (wording varies by device).
  3. Installopen the signed file on your device and confirm the install.
